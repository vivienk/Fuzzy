From a personal prompt to a place to return.

Fuzzy explores AI-generated greeting worlds for friends, couples, and families. The proposed first implementation uses AI to compose curated 3D assets, lighting, layout, and interactions. This presentation lets you explore the experience before that system is built.

V1

Concept & design

Two holiday themes, prompts, flows, and visual storyboards. APK optional.

V2

Generate & play

Prompt-driven worlds and a first playable APK, after SDK confirmation.

V3

Send & keep

Recipient delivery, saved worlds, and retrieval across sessions.

V4

Polish & demonstrate

Generation quality, performance, accessibility, and a complete APK demo.

Platform decision still to confirm

WebXR, Three.js, and React Three Fiber are the preferred prototype approach. The program asks for Unity SDK or Spatial SDK; compatibility and the APK delivery route are unconfirmed. V2–V4 require APKs.
